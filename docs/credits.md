## Data sources

- [Last.fm API](https://www.last.fm/api): scrobbling and listening history. Subject to the [Last.fm API Terms](https://www.last.fm/api/tos).
- [Discogs API](https://www.discogs.com/developers): your collection, release
  details, tracklists and artwork. Data provided by Discogs.
- [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org): track durations and release data.
  Core data CC0; requests follow the
  [rate-limiting rules](https://musicbrainz.org/doc/MusicBrainz_API/Rate_Limiting).
- [Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org): artwork for MusicBrainz
  releases.
- [Deezer API](https://developers.deezer.com): track durations, albums,
  tracklists and artwork.
- [Genius API](https://docs.genius.com): lyrics links shown as a QR code.
- [iTunes Search API](https://performance-partners.apple.com/search-api):
  artwork.
- [Shazam](https://www.shazam.com): recognition, read from its notifications.

Artwork from all sources remains the property of its rights holders.
Roomtone is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these services.
